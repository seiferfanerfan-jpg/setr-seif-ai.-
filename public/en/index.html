05 / Privacy and permissions

Clear information,
without overpromising.

This is general information, not an independent security audit. Feature details may vary by Murshid version and phone.

01
▧

Photos on your phone

Before using a feature that handles photos, review the permission and how that version processes them. Don’t share an image unless you’re comfortable doing so.

Review permissions first
02
✧

Artificial intelligence

If you use Murshid AI, your messages and the context you send may be shared with the provider to generate a reply. Review the provider’s policy and avoid sending sensitive information if you’re not comfortable.

Choose your provider carefully
03
⌑

Security and permissions

No app can be guaranteed completely secure. Give Murshid only the permissions it needs, and keep your phone’s system and the app up to date.

Stay aware of permissions
⌑

Access-key data

If you request an access key, your name, email, and a key fingerprint are stored to manage the request. The full key is shown once and cannot be recovered from the admin panel; an administrator can delete the request record.

!

App use

Don’t tamper with, modify, or redistribute the app without permission. Any rights or exceptions provided by local law remain in effect; any action will be within the law.
